About

I'm a Data Scientist with 4+ years of experience turning messy, real-world data into decisions organizations can act on — across FMCG, retail, healthcare, fintech, and EdTech. I've shipped lead-scoring models for Fortune 500 clients across three continents, improved demand forecasting for a multinational consumer goods leader, and built agentic AI copilots that sit inside a client's actual workflow, not just a slide deck.

Lately, I spend a good chunk of my time outside work prototyping full decision-intelligence products end-to-end — marketing budget optimizers, credit-risk scoring, retention-intelligence dashboards — built on predictive ML with explainability, not agentic pipelines. Most ship as static sites with a small in-browser LLM for the Q&A layer, so nobody's waiting on a backend or an API key. I care less about reaching for the fanciest model and more about whether the thing actually gets used.

I'm also open to freelance prototypes — if you have a data problem and want to see a working product before committing to a full build, that's exactly the kind of thing I like to take on.
